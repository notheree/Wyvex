Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who we are

WYVEX Ltd operates as an AI solutions provider for small businesses. Our registered address is in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at hello@wyvex.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

When you submit our contact form or book a call, we collect your name, email address, and any information you voluntarily provide. We also collect standard website analytics data (pages visited, time on site) via Google Analytics.

3. How we use your data

We use your information to respond to enquiries, arrange discovery calls, and send relevant service information. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@wyvex.co.uk.

6. Cookies

We use cookies to improve user experience and analyse site usage. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice on our website.
